Weekly Relogifact

"1729 is the smallest number which can be expressed as the sum of two cubes in two different ways: 1729 = 1³ + 12³ = 9³ + 10³. This number is known as the Hardy-Ramanujan number, or Ramanujan's Constant, named after the mathematicians G.H. Hardy and Srinivasa Ramanujan."
